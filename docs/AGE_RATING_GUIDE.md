# Age Rating Guide (App Store Connect)

Use this before release submission and answer based on actual shipped build.

## Recommended approach
1. Do not under-rate the app.
2. Classify according to actual gameplay and language in production assets.
3. Re-check after every content update.

## Quick checklist
- [ ] Violence: cartoon/non-realistic vs realistic.
- [ ] Language: profanity present or removed.
- [ ] User-generated content: leaderboard names and reports.
- [ ] Online interaction: player names visible to others.
- [ ] Gambling/alcohol/drugs references: present in player-facing content or removed.

## Important
- If you keep leaderboard/user names, mark online interactions accordingly.
- If any mature references remain, choose the stricter rating.
